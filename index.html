WELCOME, UNIVERSE MAKER

Follow your curiosity.

Every planet falls toward the sun. A little sideways motion turns that fall into an orbit.

  • Drag to launch. The direction and length of your drag set a planet’s velocity. Try a short, sideways flick.
  • Start with a pattern. Harmony is calm. Bloom traces petals. Wild embraces the unexpected. Changing patterns replaces your garden.
  • Let it sing. Planets play a pentatonic note as they cross the right-hand ray from the sun. Sound starts only when you enable it.
  • Make it yours. Adjust gravity, slow down time, or save a postcard with the download button.
